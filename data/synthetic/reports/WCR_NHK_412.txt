3. DRILLING INCIDENT LOG (SECTION 5.1 - GAS KICK EVENT)
[PAGE 24]
Depth: 2,912.0 m MD | Stratigraphy: Barail Coal-Shale Formation
Incident Type: GAS KICK & INFLUX IN BARIAL SAND-COAL TRANSITION
Description of Event:
While drilling ahead at 2,912.0 m MD with mud weight 1.26 SG, the active pit volume
began increasing rapidly. Recorded an 18-barrel pit gain within 6 minutes.
Flow out paddle indicated 88% flow with pumps constant at 2,200 LPM.
The driller performed a flow check: well continued flowing with pumps off (1.5 bbl/min).

Immediate Well Control Actions Taken:
1. Space out drillstring, shut in well on Annular Blowout Preventer (BOP).
2. Monitored pressures: Initial Shut-In Drillpipe Pressure (SIDPP) = 320 psi;
   Initial Shut-In Casing Pressure (SICP) = 410 psi.
3. Calculated required kill mud weight:
   MW_kill = 1.26 SG + (320 / (0.052 * 2780 * 3.2808)) = 1.34 SG.
4. Weighted up mud system in suction pits to 1.34 SG using barite.
5. Successfully killed well using the Wait and Weight method. Zero gas reached surface.
Total NPT: 18.0 hours.

Recommendations:
- Barail formation in Greater Naharkatiya exhibits abnormal pore pressure ramps between
  2,880 m and 2,950 m MD. Pre-weight mud to 1.32 SG prior to penetrating 2,850 m.
